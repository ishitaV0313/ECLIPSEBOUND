# 🌑 ECLIPSEBOUND — Characters

> **Bound by Love. Doomed by Fate.**

ECLIPSEBOUND is built around characters whose emotions, choices, relationships, and powers are deeply connected to the cosmic balance of AETHRYX.

---

## 🌌 Main Characters

### 🟣 ISHIRA

**Role:** Protagonist  
**Realm:** VYRAEL  
**Power:** Violet Energy — VYRAEL  
**Theme:** Hope • Emotion • Destiny

Ishira is the central character of ECLIPSEBOUND.

Her birth is connected to the coming apocalypse and the mysterious forces surrounding the Lunar Eclipse. She possesses a rare connection to VYRAEL, allowing her emotions to interact with cosmic energy.

Ishira's journey is not simply about becoming powerful. It is about discovering who she is, understanding the truth behind her birth, and deciding whether destiny must truly be obeyed.

**Core Conflict:**  
Can someone born from the fate of the universe choose her own fate?

---

### 🔴 VAEL

**Role:** Ancient Dragon / Major Character  
**Realm:** AETHRYX  
**Power:** Cosmic Dragon Energy  
**Theme:** Destruction • Instinct • Fate

Vael is one of the most powerful beings connected to AETHRYX.

His existence is closely tied to the Lunar Eclipse and the ancient forces that threaten the balance between the realms.

Vael represents the terrifying side of cosmic power — but beneath his strength lies a deeper connection to the people and events surrounding Ishira.

**Core Conflict:**  
Is Vael destined to destroy the world, or can his choices change the fate written for him?

---

### ⚔️ CIELLE

**Role:** Knight / Companion  
**Realm:** Unknown  
**Power:** Combat • Discipline • Willpower  
**Theme:** Loyalty • Courage • Humanity

Cielle is a skilled knight who becomes an important part of Ishira's journey.

Unlike beings born with extraordinary cosmic abilities, Cielle relies on training, determination, and an unbreakable sense of loyalty.

Their relationship with Ishira becomes one of the emotional foundations of the story.

**Core Conflict:**  
How far can loyalty survive when the truth becomes dangerous?

---

### 🖤 VEYRIK

**Role:** Major Character  
**Realm:** Unknown  
**Power:** Unknown  
**Theme:** Love • Strength • Sacrifice

Veyrik is one of the most emotionally significant characters in the ECLIPSEBOUND universe.

He is strong enough to stand against Vael, yet gentle enough to understand Ishira.

His relationship with Ishira becomes increasingly important as the story develops.

Veyrik is not simply defined by his strength. His greatest conflict comes from the choices he must make between love, loyalty, and the fate of the universe.

> **"Strong enough to stand against Vael. Soft enough to love Ishira. Unique enough to be remembered."**

**Core Conflict:**  
What happens when protecting someone you love means standing against destiny itself?

# 💜 Character Philosophy

Characters in ECLIPSEBOUND are not defined only by their abilities.

Their emotions influence their decisions, relationships influence their destiny, and their choices can affect the balance between entire realms.

### The central idea:

> **In ECLIPSEBOUND, emotion is power — and every choice has a consequence.**
