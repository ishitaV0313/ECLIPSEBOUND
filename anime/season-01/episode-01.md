# 🌌 CORE IDEA

Episode 1 serves as the **cosmic beginning of ECLIPSEBOUND**.

The episode introduces the first major events that will eventually shape the entire universe:

- 🌑 The first **Lunar Eclipse**
- 🐉 The birth of **VAEL** in AETHRYX
- 🌍 The reaction of **LUMERIS**
- 🔮 The first prophecy surrounding the coming imbalance
- 💜 The beginning of **ISHIRA'S existence**
- 🌌 The first connection between Vael, Ishira, and the cosmic balance

> **The episode does not rush.**
>
> Everything feels ancient.
> Everything feels inevitable.
>
> The audience should feel that something enormous has begun—even if they do not yet understand what it means.

---

# 🌑 ACT I — THE SKY CHANGES

### SCENE 01 — SILENCE BEFORE

**[EXT. COSMOS — NIGHT]**

Stars...

Still.

Too still.

The sound of the universe gradually fades.

The screen remains almost completely silent.

An ancient, calm voice begins to speak.

> **ANCIENT VOICE**
>
> *“There was a time… when balance did not question itself.”*

The moon appears.

Beautiful.

Silent.

Untouched.

Then—

A shadow slowly begins to consume it.

---

## 🌘 THE LUNAR ECLIPSE BEGINS

The universe begins behaving differently.

- Light bends.
- Sound distorts.
- Time feels slower.
- Stars appear to shift.
- The cosmic atmosphere becomes unnaturally still.

The eclipse deepens.

> **ANCIENT VOICE**
>
> *“And then… something chose to be born.”*

**CUT TO BLACK.**

---

# 🐉 ACT II — AETHRYX

## SCENE 02 — REALM OF BALANCE

**[EXT. AETHRYX]**

AETHRYX.

The living cosmic system at the center of the universe.

Floating lands drift through an endless cosmic sky.

Rivers of light flow between enormous structures.

Everything appears perfectly symmetrical.

Balanced.

Calm.

Then—

Something changes.

A disturbance moves through the realm.

The eclipse is felt.

---

## 🐉 SCENE 03 — THE DRAGONS AWAKEN

### THE FIRST GREAT VISUAL MOMENT

Across AETHRYX, dragons begin to awaken.

Not one.

Not a few.

**All of them.**

### 🐉 CELESTIAL DRAGONS

Massive creatures formed from cosmic light.

Their bodies glow against the darkness.

They remain almost completely silent.

---

### 🐲 VOID DRAGONS

Dark, shifting creatures whose forms appear to distort the space around them.

Their silhouettes move unnaturally.

---

### 💎 CRYSTAL DRAGONS

Radiant dragons covered in sharp crystalline structures.

Their bodies reflect the strange eclipse light.

---

### ⚡ STORM DRAGONS

Ancient beasts surrounded by lightning.

Thunder rolls across AETHRYX.

---

### 🐉 ELDER DRAGONS

The oldest beings of the realm.

Ancient.

Massive.

Watching.

---

One by one—

Their eyes open.

Then—

**ROARS.**

The sound travels across AETHRYX.

But these are not roars of rage.

They are roars of **recognition**.

Something has entered existence.

Something that should not be possible.

---

# 🐉 SCENE 04 — THE DRAGON COUNCIL

Massive ancient dragons gather at the heart of AETHRYX.

Their presence alone changes the atmosphere.

Silence.

Then one elder dragon speaks.

> **ELDER DRAGON**
>
> *“This is not balance.”*

Another dragon responds.

> **SECOND ELDER**
>
> *“Something has crossed the threshold.”*

The others remain silent.

The eclipse continues above them.

---

# 🌑 SCENE 05 — BIRTH OF VAEL

At the center of AETHRYX—

Reality begins to distort.

Space bends.

Light collapses inward.

The cosmic energy surrounding the distortion becomes unstable.

Everything goes silent.

Then—

**A CHILD APPEARS.**

Silence.

The dragons do not attack.

They simply observe.

The newborn opens his eyes.

One eye reflects light.

The other reflects something deeper.

Something unknown.

The dragons realize what they are witnessing.

> **ELDER DRAGON**
>
> *“He carries both ends.”*

A pause.

> **SECOND ELDER**
>
> *“Then he should not exist.”*

The child remains silent.

His name is revealed:

# VAEL

The first existence born from the fracture of the balance.

---

# 🌕 ACT III — LUMERIS

## SCENE 06 — THE KINGDOM OF LIGHT

**[EXT. LUMERIS — NIGHT]**

A golden kingdom stretches beneath the eclipsed sky.

Floating temples.

Radiant architecture.

Golden towers.

A peaceful civilization built around order, knowledge, and light.

The people look toward the sky.

The eclipse has become impossible to ignore.

Some kneel.

Some pray.

Others simply stare.

> **CITIZEN**
>
> *“The Lunar Eclipse…”*

Another whispers:

> **CITIZEN**
>
> *“A sacred sign.”*

---

# 🧙 SCENE 07 — MONKS DISCUSS

**[INT. TEMPLE HALL — NIGHT]**

Ancient monks gather inside a vast ceremonial chamber.

The eclipse shines through enormous windows.

The atmosphere is tense.

> **MONK 01**
>
> *“The balance has shifted.”*

Silence.

> **MONK 02**
>
> *“Something was born.”*

The monks exchange uneasy looks.

---

# 🧙‍♂️ SCENE 08 — MASTER SORYU ELTHAN

At the center of the chamber sits **MASTER SORYU ELTHAN**.

His eyes are closed.

He remains completely still.

The others wait.

Finally—

> **SORYU**
>
> *“…Not something.”*

He opens his eyes.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“…Someone.”*

The room falls silent.

One monk steps forward.

> **MONK**
>
> *“A blessing?”*

Soryu looks toward the eclipsed moon.

A long pause.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“…No.”*

Another pause.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“A question.”*

---

# 🌌 SCENE 09 — PROPHECY HINT

Soryu looks toward the sky.

His expression changes.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“When balance begins to take form…”*
>
> *“the world must prepare to lose it.”*

The eclipse continues.

**CUT.**

---

# 🌿 ACT IV — VYRAEL REALM

## SCENE 10 — A DIFFERENT ENERGY

**[EXT. VYRAEL REALM — NIGHT]**

VYRAEL is unlike AETHRYX.

This realm feels alive.

Unstable.

Powerful.

Storms move across the sky.

Violet energy pulses through the land.

The eclipse reaches even here.

Something is changing.

---

# 👑 SCENE 11 — AURELYON PALACE

**[INT. AURELYON PALACE — ROYAL CHAMBER]**

Soft violet light fills the room.

But the atmosphere is tense.

The Queen lies resting.

She appears weak.

Beside her stands **KING KAEL VYRAETH**.

His expression is filled with concern.

A healer approaches.

---

# 🤍 SCENE 12 — THE QUEEN

> **HEALER**
>
> *“The child… is stable.”*

Kael exhales slightly.

But the healer hesitates.

> **HEALER**
>
> *“But the energy around her…”*

He stops.

Kael looks at him.

> **KAEL VYRAETH**
>
> *“What is it?”*

The healer struggles to explain.

> **HEALER**
>
> *“It’s not natural.”*

Silence.

---

# 💜 SCENE 13 — FIRST CONNECTION

The Queen slowly places her hand over her stomach.

For a brief moment—

**A faint violet pulse appears.**

The Queen gasps softly.

She looks down.

Something inside her has responded.

> **QUEEN**
>
> *“…She felt that.”*

The room falls silent.

The child is still unborn.

But something has already awakened.

---

# 🌑 FINAL ACT — CROSS CONNECTION

## SCENE 14 — PARALLEL CUT

The episode begins connecting the three moments.

### VAEL

Standing within AETHRYX.

He looks toward the eclipsed sky.

---

### SORYU

Meditating within the temple of LUMERIS.

His eyes remain fixed upon the moon.

---

### THE QUEEN

Holding her unborn child within the palace of VYRAEL.

Her hand remains over her stomach.

A faint violet light pulses beneath it.

---

The three images begin to overlap.

The eclipse fills the screen.

Silence.

Then—

> **NARRATOR**
>
> *“One was born to question balance…”*

**VAEL'S EYE OPENS.**

> *“One had yet to be born to change it…”*

**VIOLET LIGHT PULSES WITHIN THE QUEEN.**

> *“And the world…”*

The eclipse reaches totality.

> *“…was not ready for either.”*
