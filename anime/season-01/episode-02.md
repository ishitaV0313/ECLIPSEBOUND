# 🌑 ECLIPSEBOUND — SEASON 1

## EPISODE 02 — "When the World Began to Burn"

> **Runtime:** 70–80 minutes  
> **Status:** Canon  
> **Episode Type:** Main Story  
> **Arc:** The Apocalypse / Birth of Ishira

---

# 🌌 CORE IDEA

Many years have passed since the Lunar Eclipse.

The universe has continued moving forward, but the balance has never truly recovered.

AETHRYX remains unstable.

The realms begin experiencing strange disturbances.

And within VYRAEL, a child is approaching the moment of her birth.

The Apocalypse Event begins.

But at the center of the destruction comes something impossible:

**ISHIRA is born.**

Not gently.

Not peacefully.

But through cosmic chaos.

> **This episode marks the origin of change.**

# 🌌 ACT I — THE WAITING YEARS

## SCENE 01 — OPENING

**[EXT. MULTIPLE REALMS — MONTAGE]**

Years have passed since the Lunar Eclipse.

The moon has returned.

The skies appear normal.

But something beneath the surface has changed.

The universe never truly recovered.

The camera moves through the different realms.

---

### 🔴 AETHRYX

VAEL has grown.

He remains silent.

Observant.

Watching.

The ancient dragons continue to monitor the strange changes within their realm.

---

### 🌕 LUMERIS

The monks continue studying the sky.

The Eclipse remains an unanswered mystery.

Ancient texts are examined.

Prophecies are compared.

The monks are still searching for the meaning behind what happened.

---

### ⚫ NOXARA

Whispers spread through the realm.

Something is changing.

Something is coming.

The shadows seem to know before anyone else.

---

### 🟣 VYRAEL

Storms intensify.

Violet energy pulses through the realm.

The atmosphere becomes increasingly unstable.

---

The ancient voice returns.

> **VOICE-OVER**
>
> *“Time did not stop after the moon broke…”*

Pause.

> *“It only waited.”*

The camera moves toward VYRAEL.

> *“Because something else… was coming.”*

---

# 👑 SCENE 02 — AURELYON PALACE

**[INT. AURELYON PALACE — ROYAL CHAMBER]**

The Queen is heavily pregnant.

The atmosphere inside the palace is tense.

A healer examines her.

The energy surrounding the child continues increasing.

> **HEALER**
>
> *“The child's energy is… increasing.”*

King Kael remains beside the Queen.

He looks toward the healer.

> **KAEL**
>
> *“That's not what worries me.”*

The room becomes silent.

Something is wrong.

---

# 🌑 ACT II — SIGNS OF COLLAPSE

## SCENE 03 — THE REALMS DESTABILIZE

The disturbance spreads across the universe.

The changes begin subtly.

Then they become impossible to ignore.

### 🌍 ACROSS THE REALMS

- Earth cracks.
- The sky flickers.
- Gravity distorts.
- Creatures behave strangely.
- Ancient energy begins resurfacing.

The balance between the realms begins breaking apart.

---

## 🌌 ASTRYNTH

The stars begin shifting unnaturally.

Constellations move.

Celestial patterns that have remained unchanged for centuries begin rearranging themselves.

Something is interfering with the natural order of the cosmos.

---

## 🌿 ELDERVAEL

Nature begins growing uncontrollably.

Roots break through ancient structures.

Trees expand rapidly.

The realm appears to be reacting to an unseen force.

---

## 🌑 NOXARA

Illusions begin leaking into reality.

Things that should only exist within the realm's shadows begin appearing in the physical world.

The boundary between illusion and reality weakens.

---

# 🌕 SCENE 04 — LUMERIS REACTION

**[INT. TEMPLE OF LUMERIS]**

The monks panic.

For the first time, the people of LUMERIS lose their sense of certainty.

One monk speaks.

> **MONK**
>
> *“This is not a blessing…”*

Silence.

Master Soryu looks toward the changing sky.

His expression is serious.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“No…”*

Pause.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“This is not the sky changing…”*

He looks toward the Eclipse.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“…this is something forcing it to change.”*

---

# 💥 ACT III — THE APOCALYPSE

## SCENE 05 — FULL CHAOS

### THE LARGEST VISUAL SEQUENCE OF THE EPISODE

The universe erupts into chaos.

Storms spread across the realms.

The skies change.

Light and shadow begin clashing unnaturally.

The ground fractures.

Energy surges through the environment.

The Apocalypse Event has begun.

---

## 🐉 AETHRYX

The dragons awaken again.

But this time—

They do not roar in recognition.

They roar in **warning**.

Their voices echo throughout the realm.

An ancient dragon looks toward the sky.

> **ELDER DRAGON**
>
> *“This is not balance…”*

The dragons recognize that something far more dangerous than the previous Eclipse is unfolding.

---

# 🏰 SCENE 06 — AURELYON COLLAPSING

**[INT. AURELYON PALACE]**

The palace begins shaking.

Walls crack.

Energy surges through the royal chamber.

The Queen screams in pain.

The healers rush toward her.

> **HEALER**
>
> *“It's too early—!”*

Kael looks toward the Queen.

Despite the chaos, his expression becomes strangely calm.

> **KAEL**
>
> *“No…”*

Pause.

> **KAEL**
>
> *“It's right on time.”*

Another violent surge shakes the palace.

---

# 💜 ACT IV — THE BIRTH OF ISHIRA

## SCENE 07 — SILENCE BEFORE BIRTH

Suddenly—

Everything slows.

The chaos disappears.

Sound fades.

The world becomes silent.

A heartbeat begins.

**THUMP.**

...

**THUMP.**

...

**THUMP.**

The heartbeat grows louder.

---

# 👶 SCENE 08 — THE MOMENT

The birth begins.

As **ISHIRA** enters the world—

Something impossible happens.

Across **ALL REALMS**, everything stops.

### 🔴 AETHRYX

Storms stop.

### 🌕 LUMERIS

Light freezes.

### ⚫ NOXARA

The shadows become still.

### 🌌 ASTRYNTH

The stars stop moving.

### 🌿 ELDERVAEL

Nature freezes mid-motion.

### 🟣 VYRAEL

The entire realm falls silent.

For one brief moment—

**TIME PAUSES.**

Then—

# 💥 A MASSIVE PULSE

Violet energy erupts from VYRAEL.

The energy spreads outward like a cosmic wave.

It crosses the boundaries between realms.

The universe feels it.

---

# 🌌 IMPACT ACROSS THE REALMS

## 🔴 AETHRYX

VAEL looks toward the sky.

For the first time—

He reacts emotionally.

His expression changes.

Something has reached him.

Something he cannot explain.

---

## 🌕 LUMERIS

Soryu suddenly gasps.

His eyes widen.

He looks toward the sky.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“…She has arrived.”*

---

## ⚫ NOXARA

The whispers stop.

Every illusion becomes silent.

For the first time, NOXARA experiences absolute stillness.

---

## 🌌 ASTRYNTH

The stars begin realigning.

The cosmic patterns shift into a new configuration.

---

# 👶 SCENE 09 — ISHIRA

**[INT. AURELYON PALACE — ROYAL CHAMBER]**

Silence.

The Queen lies exhausted.

Then—

A newborn cries.

Soft.

But powerful.

The room trembles slightly with each breath.

The Queen weakly smiles.

She looks at her child.

> **QUEEN**
>
> *“…She's… not normal.”*

Kael looks at Ishira.

His expression is calm, but his eyes reveal that he already understands something is different.

> **KAEL**
>
> *“…She was never meant to be.”*

The camera slowly moves toward Ishira.

---

# 🌑 ACT V — AFTERMATH

## SCENE 10 — THE TRUTH BEGINS

The destruction begins to settle.

But the universe has changed.

Across the realms, those capable of sensing the cosmic disturbance begin to understand what happened.

---

### 🌕 LUMERIS

Soryu stands beneath the changed sky.

He speaks quietly.

> **SORYU**
>
> *“One was born under silence…”*

Pause.

> *“The other… under collapse.”*

He looks toward the horizon.

> *“And when they meet…”*

He stops.

A long silence.

> *“…the world will not remain the same.”*

---

# 🖤 FINAL SCENE

**[INT. AURELYON PALACE]**

Ishira lies quietly in her mother's arms.

Her eyes remain closed.

Then—

Her eyes open.

For a split second—

A faint violet flicker appears.

Then—

Normal again.

The screen slowly fades into darkness.

---

# 🖤 FINAL LINE

> *“She was not born into the world…”*
>
> *“The world was reborn… around her.”*
