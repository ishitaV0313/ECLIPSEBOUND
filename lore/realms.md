# 🌌 ECLIPSEBOUND — Realms

> Seven realms. One cosmic balance. One fate bound by the Eclipse.

## 🌑 Overview

The universe of ECLIPSEBOUND is divided into seven interconnected realms. Each realm possesses its own identity, energy, culture, and relationship with the cosmic system known as **AETHRYX**.

The balance between these realms is essential to the survival of the universe.

---

## 🔴 1. AETHRYX

**Theme:** Origin • Cosmic Balance • Dragons  
**Primary Color:** Red

AETHRYX is the living cosmic system at the center of the ECLIPSEBOUND universe.

It is more than a realm—it is a living force that connects and influences the other realms.

AETHRYX is also the birthplace of **VAEL**, the dragon whose existence becomes deeply connected to the coming Eclipse.

---

## ⚫ 2. NOXARA

**Theme:** Darkness • Shadows • Secrets  
**Primary Color:** Black

NOXARA is a realm shaped by darkness and hidden knowledge.

Its inhabitants understand forces that remain unknown to most other realms, making NOXARA one of the most mysterious regions of AETHRYX.

---

## 🟡 3. LUMERIS

**Theme:** Light • Knowledge • Civilization  
**Primary Color:** Gold

LUMERIS represents light, knowledge, and civilization.

The realm stands in contrast to NOXARA and plays an important role in maintaining balance between opposing forces.

---

## 🟣 4. VYRAEL

**Theme:** Emotion • Power • Destiny  
**Primary Color:** Violet

VYRAEL is deeply connected to emotional energy.

It is the realm associated with **ISHIRA**, whose birth becomes one of the most significant events in ECLIPSEBOUND.

The mysterious violet energy known as **VYRAEL** is connected to the power of emotion and its ability to influence destiny.

---

## 🔵 5. ASTRYNTH

**Theme:** Stars • Time • Cosmic Knowledge  
**Primary Color:** Blue

ASTRYNTH is associated with the stars and the deeper mysteries of the cosmos.

Its connection to celestial forces gives it an important role in understanding the larger cosmic structure of ECLIPSEBOUND.

---

## 🟢 6. ELDERVAEL

**Theme:** Ancient Forces • Nature • Memory  
**Primary Color:** Green

ELDERVAEL is an ancient realm connected to nature, forgotten knowledge, and forces that existed long before the current age.

The realm preserves memories and secrets from earlier eras of the universe.

---

## 🩸 7. NYX'VAELOR

**Theme:** Forbidden Knowledge • Chaos • Destruction  
**Status:** Forbidden Realm

NYX'VAELOR is the forbidden realm.

Very little is known about its true nature.

Its existence is connected to forces that could disrupt the balance between the other realms, making it one of the greatest mysteries and dangers within ECLIPSEBOUND.

---

# ⚖️ The Balance

The seven realms are not independent.

They are connected through **AETHRYX**, and disturbances in one realm can influence the others.

The coming **Lunar Eclipse** threatens to disrupt this balance.

The consequences will ultimately lead to the birth of **ISHIRA** and the beginning of a new era.

---

> **"When the Eclipse awakens, the realms will remember what they were meant to forget."**
